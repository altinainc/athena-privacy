Privacy Policy for Athena

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Athena ("the app") is a music player client for personal media servers (Subsonic/Navidrome-compatible) and local device music. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app collects

Athena does not operate its own backend server and does not collect, store, or sell your personal data on any server we control. Data handled by the app falls into these categories:

1. Your media server credentials

If you connect Athena to your own Subsonic/Navidrome-compatible media server, the server address, username, and password you provide are stored securely on your device only (using Android's encrypted credential storage) and are sent directly to the server you specify, to authenticate your requests. This data is never sent to Athena's developers or any third party.

2. Local device music library

With your permission, Athena reads your device's local music library (via Android's MediaStore) to let you browse and play music stored on your device. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

3. Third-party metadata and services

To show album art, artist images, and recommendations, Athena may send artist/album/track names to the following third-party services. No personal information or account credentials are sent to these services — only music metadata needed to fetch artwork or metadata:

  • MusicBrainz
  • Cover Art Archive
  • ListenBrainz
  • ReccoBeats
  • Wikipedia

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle requests made to them.

4. Casting

Athena supports casting playback to Chromecast and DLNA devices on your local network. This uses local network discovery only and does not send data outside your network beyond the media stream to the selected device.

5. Purchases

Athena offers an optional one-time "Pro" unlock via Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play and is never seen by Athena or its developers.

Data sharing

Athena does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data deletion

You can remove all locally stored data (server credentials, cached metadata, downloaded tracks) by clearing the app's data/storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Athena is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
